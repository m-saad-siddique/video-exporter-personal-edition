# Community conduct

This project welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.

- Treat people respectfully and focus feedback on the work.
- Discuss technical disagreements constructively, with examples where possible.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or publish private information about others.
- Keep issues, pull requests, and discussions relevant to the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive or off-topic contributions, lock conversations, or restrict participation when needed to keep the project welcoming.

Use GitHub's **Report content** controls to report abuse on the platform. Security vulnerabilities should be reported through the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
